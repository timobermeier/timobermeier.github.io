About

I am a Lecturer in Economics at the University of Leicester, a Research Associate at the Institute for Fiscal Studies and an Associate at the Centre for Economic Performance. I hold a PhD from the University of Mannheim. My research is in macroeconomics, with a focus on labour markets, family decision-making and inequality.

You can reach me at {{ site.author.email }}. Students can book the office hour for EC2051: Money and Central Banking or a personal tutor meeting here. You can find my CV here.
